Your privacy with Sherpy

This is a hackathon prototype that runs on your own computer. The demo families are fictitious. A real launch would also need the agreements and records the law asks for (see the last section).

What Sherpy uses

  • Your plan: the Sherpas financial plan of your family (figures, goals, recommendations).
  • Your answers in the conversation: who is joining, your main goal, your biggest worry, how each of you reacts to risk, whether you prefer numbers or what they mean, and how much detail you want.
  • Your voice, live, only while a conversation or the Sherpy bubble is open.
  • What you type in the missions (for example your insurer, or the order of your priorities).

Who processes it

  • Live conversation and the Sherpy bubble: your voice and Sherpy's replies stream through this app's server to the Sherpas hackathon AI service, which uses Google Gemini. Sherpy also receives the plan figures it needs to answer you.
  • Your video: the plan figures and your answers (including first names) go to the same AI service, where an OpenAI model writes the narration and ElevenLabs reads it aloud.
  • The secret keys for these services stay on the server; your browser never sees them.

What is kept, where, and for how long

  • No audio is recorded or stored. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising.
  • On this computer (server): your conversation answers (data/profiles) and your built video (data/videos), until you delete them. The Sherpy bubble remembers the last chat in memory for up to 2 hours, and forgets it when the server restarts.
  • Transcripts of what was said are off by default. If a developer turns them on, they are deleted automatically after 7 days. Archived copies are deleted after 30 days.
  • In your browser only: your mission progress and what you typed in the missions.
  • The video page loads its font from Google Fonts, so Google sees that request.

Your choices

  • See your data: it is stored as plain files on this computer, in the folders above.
  • Delete your data: in the agent folder run npm run data:delete -- <case> --yes, and open reset.html to clear what this browser saved.
  • Stop at any time: end the conversation or close the bubble; the voice connection closes at once.

Before a real launch

Using this with real families would need, at least: a clear legal basis and consent screen, data processing agreements with the AI providers, a record of processing, a data protection impact assessment (it handles financial data), and a named contact for privacy requests. We have not done these legal steps.
